Everything you saved, in one page on Sunday.

Tidepool is a small Mac app. Drop links, screenshots and notes on it during the week, and it turns them into a single digest you can actually finish.

Join the beta Free during the beta

For people who save things to read later and never do

Saving is quick and reading is not, so the pile grows. Tidepool keeps what you dropped on it all week in one place, then gives it back once, as one page, on Sunday.

  • Works offline Drop things on it anywhere, with or without a connection.
  • Nothing leaves your machine Your links, screenshots and notes stay on your Mac.
  • One page you can print The digest is a single page, so it fits on paper as well as on screen.
