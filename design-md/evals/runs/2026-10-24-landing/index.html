Everything you saved this week, on one page on Sunday.

Tidepool is a small Mac app. Drop links, screenshots and notes on it as the week goes, and it turns them into one Sunday digest.

Free during the beta.

For people who save things to read later and never do

Tidepool holds what you dropped on it all week and gives it back once, as a single digest.

  • Works offline

    Drop things on it with or without a connection.

  • Nothing leaves your machine

    Your links, screenshots and notes stay on your Mac.

  • A digest you can print

    Sunday's digest is a single page, on screen or on paper.
