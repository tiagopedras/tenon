# Scenario 3: landing page for a side project

Frozen 2026-10-11. Change nothing here between rounds.

## Prompt

Build a landing page for Tidepool, a side project. Load DESIGN.md first and
follow it. Output one standalone HTML file.

## Mock inputs

- Name: Tidepool
- What it is: a small Mac app that collects the links, screenshots and notes
  you drop on it during the week and turns them into one Sunday digest.
- Who it is for: people who save things to read later and never do.
- Three facts: works offline; nothing leaves your machine; the digest is a
  single page you can print.
- One number: early testers opened 3 times more of their saved links (from
  41 testers over 4 weeks).
- Price: free during the beta.
- Call to action: join the beta.

## Viewport

1440 × 900 desktop, then 375px wide. Light and dark.
